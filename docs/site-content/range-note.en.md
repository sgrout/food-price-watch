**Why so wide?** Food prices really move this much, especially around the harvest and the lean season, and WFP
publishes prices one to several months late, so "next month" is often two or three months after the last known price. A
narrower range would look more precise but be wrong more often. Use the most likely value for the direction and
the upper end as a margin. [More about the ranges](/methods/#why-the-ranges-are-wide)
