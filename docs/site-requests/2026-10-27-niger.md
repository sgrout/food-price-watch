Status: accepted (site, 2026-10-29): 1.5 and basket-1.1 accepted; ready for Niger
From: data side, 2026-10-27, on the owner's go

## Request
The owner gave the go for **Niger** (`niger`, currency XOF, French "Niger"). Chad is downloaded but **not**
forecast or exported (WFP publishes it 2-4 months late), so there is nothing to build for Chad.

**Rollout (please keep this order):**
1. Accept **schema 1.5** (item) and **basket-1.1** (basket) next to the current versions (copy `export/schema/`). The
   only schema change: country ids are any slug (`^[a-z]+(-[a-z]+)*$`) instead of a fixed list, so later countries
   need no schema change; plus `countries.json` `note` (`{en, fr}` or `null`).
2. Tell us here (Answer) when that is live. **Only then the data side adds Niger to the exports** (bundle variable
   `public_countries`); until then the exports stay 1.4 / basket-1.0 with three countries.
3. Trial exports with Niger, in the volume `fpw_dev_gold.public_export_next` (`latest.tar.gz` and
   `baskets/latest.tar.gz`; not for the live site), if you want to test with full data: 1,397 series (Niger 215, 190
   with a forecast, 53 markets), 264 baskets (Niger 53, millet only), 1.8 MB.

**Pages:** nothing new beyond what exists, but please check:
- the Niger country page, its market × food pages (about 215 + 53 basket pages per language, about 540 in all) and
  the home page list (now four countries); budgets and link checks;
- `countries.json` `note`: show it on the country page (Niger), near the track record numbers, as plain text with a
  link to the track record; `null` for the other countries;
- `track_record.json` `backtest.by_country.niger` exists (and the basket one); the pooled numbers already include Niger's
  backtest cases;
- re-copy `site-content/about.{en,fr}.md` (now "Mali, Burkina Faso, Burundi and Niger").

## Example
Samples: `export/sample/series/niger-601-64-retail-kg.json`, `export/sample/countries.json` (Niger's `note`),
