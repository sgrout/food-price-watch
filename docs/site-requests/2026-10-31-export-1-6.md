# Export 1.6: forecasts from the freshest published prices

Status: open (for the site side: first accept 1.6, then say so here)
From: data side, 2026-10-31, on the owner's decision

## Request
The product model changes from `lgbm_cal` to `lgbm_fresh2`: each forecast starts from the newest month WFP has
published for that country, instead of a fixed "two months ago". Better forecasts and narrower ranges, with an honest
track record. Details: contract §3 "Changes in 1.6".

**Rollout (please keep this order):**
1. Accept **schema 1.6** next to 1.5 (copy `export/schema/`; basket schemas are unchanged, still `basket-1.1`).
2. Make the changes below, and tell us here (Answer). **Only then the data side switches the weekly export**; until
   then it stays 1.5. A trial 1.6 export is in `fpw_dev_gold.public_export_next` (`latest.tar.gz`; not for the live site):
   1,393 series, 1,094 with a forecast, 1.7 MB. (Its `baskets/` folder there is older and not part of this trial.)

**What the site must change:**
- **`data_up_to` per country.** Use `countries.json` `data_up_to` on country pages and `dates.data_up_to` on series pages.
  Top-level files (`manifest`, indexes, `track_record`) carry the **newest across countries**, so do not show that as
  every country's date. Today: Burkina Faso, Burundi, Niger 2026-09; Mali 2026-08.
- **`forecast[]` has 4 or 5 rows.** A month already published has no estimate, so `nowcast_last_month` can be missing. Find
  rows by `horizon`, never by position; the chart and table must work with either. The fan starts at
  `last_observed_month`, as before.
- **A market can be behind its country's `data_up_to`.** In Burundi only some markets have September; most end in August
  (`last_observed_month`), while `reference_month` says August. That is normal: show both dates as they are.
- **Track record text.** `track_record.json` `backtest.period` is now "2023 onwards, months with known publication dates":
  show it as is instead of a fixed "2023-01 onwards". Basket track record is unchanged.
- **New-spike verdict.** `new_spike_verdict` is now `better` up to 2 months ahead (`no_better` at 3 months). Keep
  the sentence "Warnings of new spikes have no proven skill yet" as it is: the owner has not changed it, and
  it stays until the live record shows it.
- **A country with no forecasts** (WFP more than 4 months behind) keeps its pages: all series `eligible: false`, history only.
  Nothing to build, but check that a country page without forecasts still renders. It does not occur in the trial.
- **Re-copy** `site-content/methods.{en,fr}.md`, `about.{en,fr}.md`, `range-note.{en,fr}.md`: the delay is now "one to
  several months" and differs per country.
